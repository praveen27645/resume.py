# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on what’s best for the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take appropriate action.

## Contact
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue or contact the maintainer.
